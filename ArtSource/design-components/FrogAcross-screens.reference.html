Gameplay
THE GOAL

Get your character from the near bank to an empty goal bay on the far side. Fill every bay on the level to finish it. One touch from anything lethal and the run restarts from the bank.

LEVELS & TIMING

100 levels, each a fixed board — same lanes, same traffic pattern, every time. The clock starts on your first move and stops when the last bay fills. Beat the level to unlock the next; beat the target times for bronze, silver or gold.

GOLD 24.0s SILVER 31.0s BRONZE 40.0s
SWIPING

Swipe anywhere on the board — there are no buttons. One swipe is one hop in that direction, and your character turns to face it. Hold a swipe at 43–47° to cover two squares in a single move. Swipes queue, so you can chain hops faster than the animation plays.
